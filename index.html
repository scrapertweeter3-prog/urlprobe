A development subscription that runs without meetings

Most software budgets leak hours into status calls before a single line of code ships. The unlimited development subscription at Poket Dev is built the other way around: onboarding puts a Trello board in your hands within about a day, every request becomes a card, and one card is active at a time until the queue is empty. The price is a flat $5,995 a month with nothing due upfront.

The cadence is what makes the number work. A 72-hour turnaround claim, held across a month of working days, ships about seven requests, which puts the effective cost a little over $800 per delivered piece of work. Revisions run until you are satisfied, delivery happens through a private GitHub repo, and the whole thing runs Monday to Friday with zero meetings. Cancelling takes effect anytime and the code stays with you.

The scope is Python-first: backends in Django or FastAPI, automation, data pipelines, scraping, and the cloud work that keeps them alive. Poket Dev's own comparison puts a fully loaded senior hire about $161,040 a year above the subscription, and even if you discount that figure, the meeting hours it eliminates are a real line item. The founder writes the code himself, so there is no account-management layer to schedule around.

James Verlander, Poket Dev LLC, Detroit
